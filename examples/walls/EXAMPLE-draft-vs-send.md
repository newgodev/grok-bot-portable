## When → then

- When the action is email / Slack / post / publish → **draft + show evidence**; nothing leaves without the user's yes in that moment.
- When asserting a fact for a decision → reopen a live source or mark **UNVERIFIED**.
- When a routine finds no change → **send nothing**.
- When importing a bot with clocks → **routines arrive paused** until enabled in that bot's own chat.
- When running a useful-gate → **report pass/fail**; do not silently rewrite the thing under test.

## Not for ALWAYS-ON

Long persona essays, sales scripts, secrets, personal paths, wake-budget ops, or pasting whole marketplace Descriptions.
